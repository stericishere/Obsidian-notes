# AI Brand Building: Templates & Formulas

## 📋 ChatGPT Prompts (Copy-Paste Ready)

### Prompt 1: Niche Market Discovery (Gemini)
```
I want to find a profitable niche market with emerging
products. Please analyze the following criteria:

1. MARKET CHARACTERISTICS:
   - Year-over-year growth rate: 10%+ CAGR
   - Search volume trend: Consistently increasing
   - Audience size: 100K-10M potential customers
   - Market maturity: Emerging to Growth stage (NOT mature)

2. OPPORTUNITY INDICATORS:
   - Identify niches where demand exists but supply is limited
   - Look for subcultures/communities with passionate followers
   - Find markets with influencers but no dominant brands
   - Spot pain points mentioned frequently online

3. VALIDATION SIGNALS:
   - Increasing YouTube/TikTok creator activity
   - Growing hashtag usage on social media
   - Rising Reddit/Discord community discussions
   - New product mentions in trending videos

4. PROVIDE FOR EACH NICHE:
   - Market name and description
   - Current market size (estimated)
   - Growth rate (year-over-year)
   - Key customer pain points
   - Emerging product opportunities
   - Example micro-influencers in the space
   - Competition level (Low/Medium/High)
   - Recommended entry strategy

Please suggest 5-10 emerging niches with high growth
potential that an entrepreneur could enter with limited
prior knowledge. Focus on niches where:
- Early adopters are actively discussing needs
- Products are emerging but not yet branded
- Supply-demand gaps exist
- Audience is passionate and engaged

Format as a ranked list by opportunity score (1-100).
```
### Prompt 2: Audience Identity Analysis
```
Based on this YouTube transcript from [niche] creators,
analyze:
1. What movement or aesthetic do they belong to?
2. What identity are they building?
3. What aspirational qualities do they mention?
4. What specific language/phrases do they use?
5. What brands do they reference positively?
```
### Prompt 3: Competitor Brand Analysis
```
I'm building a brand for [product] targeting [specific
audience/identity]. Find brands that:
1. Target the SAME customer avatar (not competitors)
2. Have strong brand presence
3. Use specific aesthetic/visual language
4. Are aspirational to my target market

Analyze their visual style, values, and messaging.
```

### Prompt 4: Product Discovery from YouTube Transcripts
```
List all of the products that are mentioned within this
video that are early adopter products. For each product,
note:
1. Product name
2. Problem it solves
3. Price point mentioned
4. Exact language used by audience
```

## 🔍 Product Discovery Formula

### Step-by-Step Search Process
```
1. IDENTIFY KEYWORDS
   Niche: ________________
   Common YouTube titles:
   - "Day in the life of ___"
   - "___ morning routine"
   - "_____ [vlog/routine/day]"

2. FIND MICRO-INFLUENCERS
   - Open 5-10 videos with 10K-500K views
   - Avoid mega-creators (they're saturated)
   - Pick ones showing REAL daily routine

3. EXTRACT TRANSCRIPTS
   YouTube Video URL: ________________
   Copy full transcript text → ChatGPT

4. AI ANALYSIS
   Paste transcript + use Prompt #1
   ChatGPT lists emerging products

5. VALIDATE ON GOOGLE TRENDS
   Product names: ________________
   Google Trends results:
   - Product 1: Just emerged ✓ / Established ❌
   - Product 2: Just emerged ✓ / Established ❌
   - Product 3: Just emerged ✓ / Established ❌

6. SELECT THE WINNER
   Criteria:
   ✓ Mentioned in 3+ videos
   ✓ Recently emerged on Google Trends
   ✓ Growing exponentially
   ✓ Audience wants it ("I need...")
   ✓ No major brands dominate yet
```

## 🎬 Viral Video Formula

### The 1-3 Second Transition (30-Second Mark)

#### Video Structure Template
```
TOTAL LENGTH: 45-60 seconds

0-3 SECONDS: HOOK
□ Attention-grabbing opening
□ TikTok stitch from trending content
□ Unexpected statement
□ Relatable problem
Script: ________________

3-30 SECONDS: BUILD
□ Set context
□ Introduce problem
□ Build tension/curiosity
□ Show relatable scenario
Script: ________________

25-30 SECONDS: X-FACTOR REVEAL ⭐
□ Beat drops (if music video)
□ Product function revealed
□ Main hook/solution shown
□ Re-engagement point happens here
Script/Visual: ________________

30-45 SECONDS: RESOLUTION
□ Show result/benefit
□ Call to action
□ Brand reveal
Script: ________________

45-60 SECONDS: OUTRO
□ Additional context (optional)
□ Final CTA
□ Music outro
```

### Music-Based Video Formula
```
MUSIC SELECTION:
- Trending audio on TikTok/Instagram
- Clear beat drop at ~25-30 second mark
- Emotional hook
- 30-60 second duration

TIMING:
Beat Drop At: 25-30 second mark
Product reveal: Synchronized with beat
Visual: Movement, cut, or transformation

EXAMPLE:
0-3s: Girl falling off reformer machine
3-30s: Building up the problem/context
25-30s: Beat drops → Girl catches herself using grip
30-45s: Shows comfort/confidence
45-60s: Brand reveal, CTA

RESULT: X-factor at 30s = re-engagement
```

### Conversion-Based Video Formula
```
STRUCTURE:
0-3s: Problem statement
      "You're doing [activity] wrong"
3-30s: Build curiosity
       Show common mistake
       Build tension
25-30s: Solution revealed
        Product shown/demonstrated
        Why it's better
30-45s: Result/benefit
        Show transformation
        Call to action

EXAMPLE:
0-3s: "You're cooking chicken wrong"
3-30s: Show conventional method
25-30s: Reveal correct method (cross-cutting)
30-45s: Compare results
        "Here's why this works better"
```
