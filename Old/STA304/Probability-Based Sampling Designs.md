**Random Selection:**
- Simple Random Sampling (SRS)
- Systematic Sampling
	- Start from random point and take every k-th unit
- Stratified Random Sampling
	- split into strata and SRS
- Cluster Sampling
	- split into clusters then SRS clusters then SRS inside the cluster

**Random Allocation**
- assigning participants who are already in the study to different treatment or control groups
- A/B testing

**Missing data**
- MCAR (Missing Completely at Random)
	- the probability of being missing is entirely unrelated to any observed or unobserved data values (Pure Chance)
- MAR (Missing at Random)
	- the probability of being missing depends on observed data (Another Column)
		- for example the 
	- Must use imputation
- MNAR (Missing Not at Random)
	- The reason the data is missing is **directly related** to the answer itself.

**Confounding variables**
- Other variable(s) not captured in the study that could affect the independent and dependent variables
	- The purpose of **random allocation** is to provide balance among confounders
- For example the strong positive correlation between snowboarding gear sales and winter tie is explained by the **Confounding variables** of weather

**Census data**
- it's the ground truth of a specific population 
	- complete count of the population

**Observational Data/Study**
- researchers **do not interfere** and there is **no random allocation**
- Capture thro observation

**Experimental Data/Experiment:**
- researchers **randomly assign** observations to treatment groups
- establish causation
- it's designed
