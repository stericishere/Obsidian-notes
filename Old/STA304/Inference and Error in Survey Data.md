- **Sampling Errors**
	- due to sample being a subset of population
- **Non-Sampling Errors**
	- Due to
	1. measurement error
		- difference between true and measured value
	2. Selection Bias
		- non-representative sample
		- Reduce with **Propensity Score Matching (PSM)**

### **Correlation vs. Causation**
- **Correlation:**
	- Implies an relationship between variables
		  - ex: ice cream sales v.s. temperature
	- any pair of variable (x1, x2) or (x1, y)
	- Correlation just says, "When one variable changes, the other tends to change too."
- **Causation:** (因果關係)
	- Causation assumes correlation exists
		- and A to B there's a cause and affect
	- It says $x_1$ **forces** $y$ to change.
	- Can find using **Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD)**

**Homoscedasticity**
- (a “fan” shape)
- That means the variance of the errors is not constant → **homoscedasticity is violated**.
