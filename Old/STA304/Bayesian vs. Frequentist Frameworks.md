The **95% Confidence Interval** (CI) relates to the long-run capture rate if the experiment is repeated
- if we make 100 experiment, there'll be 95 times the true parameter will lie within the interval

The **Bayesian** assuming a **prior distribution** and use the **prior distribution** and the data to estimate the **Posterior distribution**
95% **Credible Interval** (CrI) means there is a 95% probability of the parameter lying within the interval
