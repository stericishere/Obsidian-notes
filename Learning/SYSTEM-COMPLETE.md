- X_i ∈ {0, 1} always
- E[X_i] = P(event i occurs)
- E[X₁ + ... + Xₙ] = Σ E[X_i] (linearity holds even if dependent!)

### 3️⃣ **Augmented Data Structures (15-20% of exam)**

- New field must be locally computable (O(1) from node's own data + children)
- Update during rotations without increasing complexity

## 🚨 Common Exam Pitfalls (From Analysis of Past Exams)

❌ **Mistake 1:** Confusing single vs. double rotation
- **Fix:** Same-sign BF = single rotation. Opposite-sign = double rotation. Memorize!

❌ **Mistake 2:** Height definition ambiguity (nodes vs. edges)
- **Fix:** State your convention at exam start. Use consistently.

❌ **Mistake 3:** Indicator variable not 0/1
- **Fix:** X_i must always be 0 or 1, then E[X_i] = P(event).

❌ **Mistake 4:** Thinking BuildHeap is O(n log n)
- **Fix:** Most work at tree base; sum converges to O(n).

❌ **Mistake 5:** Only rotating first unbalanced node
- **Fix:** Check ancestors after rotation; cascade may be needed.

❌ **Mistake 6:** Assuming hashing is always O(1)
- **Fix:** O(1) only avg-case under SUHA. Worst-case is O(n).

All 6 covered in **Document 4: Quick-Reference.md** with corrections.
