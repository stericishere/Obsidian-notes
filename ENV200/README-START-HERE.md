## 🧠 The 10 Core Concepts (At a Glance)

Master these 10 concepts and you've covered ~80% of the exam:

1. **Thermodynamics** (1st & 2nd Law) → Explains trophic pyramids
2. **Feedback Loops** (Positive ≠ Good) → Core to climate systems
3. **Scientific Method** (Iterative, not linear) → Foundation for all science
4. **Greenhouse Effect** (Natural vs. Enhanced) → Mechanism of climate change
5. **Atmospheric Layers** (Troposphere vs. Stratosphere) → Different heating mechanisms
6. **Hadley Cells & Coriolis** (Why deserts at 30°) → Global circulation
7. **Demographic Transition** (Death falls first, birth lags) → Population explosion
8. **Nitrogen Cycle** (Requires bacteria) → Limiting nutrient
9. **IPAT Equation** (Impact = P × A × T) → Sustainability formula
10. **Ecological Footprint** (Demand vs. Supply) → Overshoot concept

## 🚨 The 7 Exam Traps (Memorize These)

These trip up most students. Avoid them and you gain easy points:

1. **Positive Feedback = Good** ❌ → Positive = Amplifying (often bad)
2. **Ozone Hole = Global Warming** ❌ → Different mechanisms, separate issues
3. **Seasons from Distance** ❌ → Seasons from axial tilt (Jan is closest!)
4. **Theory = Guess** ❌ → Theory = highest evidence level
5. **Rates Fall Together** ❌ → Death falls first; birth lags 30-50 years
6. **Science Proves** ❌ → Science provides strong evidence, not proof
7. **Heat Rises** ❌ → Buoyancy (density) rises; heat doesn't

**Reference [[ENV200H1S-Exam-Trap-Detector.md]] during exam if unsure.**

## 📊 Exam Format & Strategy

**Exam Format:**
- 15-20 Multiple Choice (40% of grade)
- 3-4 Short Answer (60% of grade)
- 90 minutes total

**Time Allocation:**
- MC: ~20 min (1-2 min per question)
- Short Answers: ~35 min (10-15 min per question)
- Review: ~30 min

**Strategy:**
1. Skim all questions first (2 min)
2. Answer easy MC first (builds confidence)
3. Skip hard MC; circle them; return later
4. Structure short answers: Define → Explain → Conclude
5. Use final 10 min to proofread
