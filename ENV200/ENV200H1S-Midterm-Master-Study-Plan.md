## 🔑 MUST-KNOW CORE CONCEPTS (Non-negotiable)

### 1. THERMODYNAMICS — The Engine of Everything

**What it is:**
- **1st Law (Conservation)**: Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed
- **2nd Law (Entropy)**: When energy transforms, some degrades to heat; no process is 100% efficient

**Why it matters:**
- Explains trophic pyramids (90% energy loss = few predators, many prey)
- Explains atmospheric heating (stratosphere vs. troposphere warming mechanisms differ)
- Explains why renewable energy faces limits

**Mental model:**
Think of energy as water flowing downhill. The 1st Law says the water doesn't disappear—it just changes form. The 2nd Law says some always spreads out as mist (heat) and can never be fully recaptured.

**Exam application:**
- "Why are top predators rare?" → 2nd Law (energy loss at each step)

### 2. FEEDBACK LOOPS — The Pattern of Change

**What it is:**
- **Positive (+)**: Output amplifies the input (destabilizing, runaway)
- **Negative (-)**: Output dampens the input (stabilizing, homeostatic)

**Common trap:**
"Positive = Good" — **FALSE**. Positive means *amplifying*, which is often destructive.

**Canonical examples:**

| Feedback  | Example                                                              | Outcome             |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| +Positive | Ice melts → lower albedo → more heat → more melting                  | Runaway warming     |
| -Negative | Predators increase → prey decrease → predators starve → prey recover | Oscillating balance |
| -Negative | Body temperature rises → sweating → cooling                          | Homeostasis         |

**Exam application:**
- Arctic Ice Albedo Effect = **Positive Feedback** (destabilizing climate)
- Predator-Prey Cycles = **Negative Feedback** (stabilizing population)

### 3. SCIENTIFIC METHOD — Non-Linear & Iterative

**The trap:**
Students think science is: Observation → Hypothesis → Experiment → Conclusion (end).

**The reality:**
Science is: Observe ↔ Hypothesize ↔ Test ↔ Revise ↔ Peer Review ↔ Back to Observation

**Key distinction:**
- **Hypothesis**: A testable, falsifiable prediction
- **Theory**: A broad explanation supported by vast evidence (NOT a "guess")
- "Evolution is just a theory" = **Scientifically illiterate** (it's the highest level of confirmation)

**Exam application:**
- Distinguish **Inductive reasoning** (Specific → General, forms hypotheses) from **Deductive reasoning** (General → Specific, tests hypotheses)

---

### 4. GREENHOUSE EFFECT — The Climate Mechanism

**Natural Greenhouse Effect:**
- Infrared radiation (heat) bounces off Earth's surface
- GHGs (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄) absorb this IR and re-emit it in all directions
- Some goes back down, warming the atmosphere
- **Without this**: Earth = -18°C (uninhabitable)
- **With this**: Earth = +15°C (habitable)

**Enhanced (Anthropogenic) Greenhouse Effect:**
- Humans increase CO₂ and CH₄ concentrations
- More GHGs trap more heat
- Temperature rises beyond natural equilibrium

**The Ozone Hole Trap (⚠️ EXAM TRAP):**
- Ozone hole = CFCs destroy O₃ in stratosphere
- Result = More UV-B/UV-C reaches Earth (cancer/mutation risk)
- **This does NOT cause global warming** (different radiation type, different mechanism)
- CFCs are GHGs, but the hole itself ≠ climate change

### 5. ATMOSPHERIC STRUCTURE — Layers & Their Heating

| Layer        | Altitude | Temperature Trend       | Why                                      |
| ------------ | -------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Troposphere  | 0-12 km  | Colder as you go up     | Heated FROM BELOW by Earth's radiation   |
| Stratosphere | 12-50 km | **Warmer as you go up** | Heated FROM WITHIN by ozone absorbing UV |
| Mesosphere   | 50-80 km | Colder as you go up     | Loses heat by radiation                  |

**Key insight:**
The stratosphere is *inverted* compared to troposphere. Ozone absorbs UV (high energy) and converts to heat. This makes the stratosphere **stable** (no convection).

### 6. HADLEY CELLS & CORIOLIS — Why Deserts Are at 30° Latitude

**The mechanism:**
1. **Equator**: Intense sun heat → air rises → low pressure → rains
2. **Poleward move**: Rising air moves north/south at high altitude
3. **30° latitude**: Air sinks (subsiding) → high pressure → **no rain = deserts**
4. **Coriolis effect**: Deflects winds right (N. Hemisphere) / left (S. Hemisphere)
   - Creates Trade Winds (easterly) and Westerlies (westerly)

**Exam application:**
- Why is the Sahara at 30°N? → Hadley Cell subsidence
- What wind patterns dominate mid-latitudes? → Westerlies (due to Coriolis)![[Screenshot 2026-02-10 at 02.57.58.png|382x315]]

### 7. DEMOGRAPHIC TRANSITION MODEL — Why Populations Explode Then Stabilize

| Stage | Birth Rate | Death Rate | Growth | What's Happening |
|-------|-----------|-----------|--------|------------------|
| 1 | High | High | Stable | Traditional societies, high infant mortality |
| 2 | High | **Falling** | **RAPID** | Medicine/sanitation arrive; deaths ↓ but births stay ↑ |
| 3 | **Falling** | Low | Slowing | Education/urbanization; women in workforce; births ↓ |
| 4 | Low | Low | Stable/Decline | Rich countries, aging populations |

**The trap:** Students think death rates and birth rates fall together. **NO.** Death rates fall first (medicine), creating a 30-50 year lag until births drop (education/culture).

**Exam application:**
- Why is Sub-Saharan Africa growing rapidly? → Stage 2 (deaths ↓, births ↑)
- Why is Japan aging? → Stage 4 (low births, low deaths)

### 8. NITROGEN CYCLE — The One Cycle Without an Atmospheric Reservoir (Unlike Carbon/Water)

**Key steps (bacteria-dependent):**
1. **Fixation**: N₂ gas → Ammonia (NH₃) [Legume bacteria]
2. **Nitrification**: Ammonia → Nitrite → Nitrate [Soil bacteria]
3. **Assimilation**: Plants uptake Nitrate
4. **Denitrification**: Nitrate → N₂ gas [Anaerobic bacteria]

**Why it matters:**
- Nitrogen is a limiting nutrient (plants need it, but can't use N₂ directly)
- Fertilizer runoff creates dead zones (eutrophication)
- Nitrogen fixation by legumes is ancient human agriculture

---

### 9. IPAT EQUATION — The Core Sustainability Formula

**I = P × A × T**
- **I** = Environmental Impact
- **P** = Population size
- **A** = Affluence (consumption per person, e.g., kg/year)
- **T** = Technology (environmental damage per unit of consumption)

**The insight:**
- A small population with high affluence (US) can have higher impact than large population with low affluence (India)
- To reduce impact: ↓P, ↓A, or ↓T (harder than it sounds)

**Exam application:**
- Which causes more environmental damage: 1 American or 100 Bangladeshis? → Depends on their A and T values

---

### 10. ECOLOGICAL FOOTPRINT vs. BIOCAPACITY

**Ecological Footprint**: Demand (How much land/water does humanity need to support current consumption?)
**Biocapacity**: Supply (How much land/water is actually available and productive?)

**Current status:**
- Footprint ≈ 1.7 Earth
- Biocapacity ≈ 1.0 Earth
- **Global ecological deficit** (overshoot)

## ⚠️ EXAM TRAPS & MISCONCEPTIONS (These Cost Points)

### Trap 1: "Positive Feedback = Good"
**Reality**: Positive = amplifying (often destructive). Ice albedo feedback is positive and destabilizing.

### Trap 2: "Ozone Hole = Climate Change"
**Reality**: Separate issues. Ozone hole = UV problem. Climate change = IR/heat problem.
it's on stratosphere and stratosphere is warmer as you go up

### Trap 3: "Seasons are caused by distance from Sun"
**Reality**: Caused by axial tilt. Earth is closest in January (Perihelion)—yet it's winter in N. Hemisphere.

### Trap 4: "Theory = Guess"
**Reality**: In science, a theory is the highest level of evidence. Evolution, Gravity, Germ Theory are all theories.

### Trap 5: "Death rates and birth rates fall together in Demographic Transition"
**Reality**: Death rates fall FIRST (medicine/sanitation). Birth rates lag 30-50 years (culture/education).

### Trap 6: "Science proves things"
**Reality**: Science provides strong evidence but never absolute proof. It's about approximations.

### Trap 7: "Heat rises"
**Reality**: Air rises because it's *less dense* (buoyant), not because "heat rises." Density, not heat, drives convection.
